Compassionate Community Healthcare, Preventive Wellness & Accessible Care

Welcome to the official home of ISHA CARE CLINIC INC. We are a dedicated 501(c)(3) nonprofit healthcare organization advancing patient-centered care, preventive health education, and holistic wellness support for individuals and families in McMinnville, Tennessee, and surrounding communities.

Our Enduring Commitment to McMinnville and Patient-Centered Care

At ISHA CARE CLINIC INC, we believe quality healthcare and holistic well-being should be welcoming, dignified, and accessible to every neighbor. Operating from 951 ISHA LN, MCMINNVILLE TN 37110, our clinic serves as a trusted resource for primary wellness guidance, preventive screenings, and compassionate health outreach across Warren County and Middle Tennessee.

Delivering Accessible Primary Care & Preventive Health Support

Healthy communities thrive when individuals have reliable guidance to manage their well-being before minor concerns become major challenges. ISHA CARE CLINIC INC brings together dedicated healthcare professionals, volunteers, and community advocates to offer compassionate consultations and preventive wellness services.

From routine health assessments and chronic condition education to patient navigation, our care team treats every person with respect, empathy, and clinical integrity.

Promoting Holistic Wellness, Health Literacy & Community Outreach

True well-being encompasses physical vitality, mental balance, and informed lifestyle habits. ISHA CARE CLINIC INC hosts community wellness workshops, nutrition and stress-management education, and local health outreach initiatives designed to empower families with practical everyday tools.

By collaborating with local civic groups and regional health partners, we help build a healthier, more resilient McMinnville community.
